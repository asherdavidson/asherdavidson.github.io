Asher's Budget

Asher's Budget is an envelope budgeting app with investment and retirement projections, for Android and Windows, with more platforms to come. Your budgets are kept on your device. To use a budget on more than one of your own devices, you can turn on sync: the app encrypts the budget on the device and keeps it in the app's own folder in your Dropbox account, or, on a computer, in a folder you choose. There is no account with us and no server of ours.
