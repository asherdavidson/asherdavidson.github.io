Asher's Budget privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026

This policy covers the Asher's Budget app on every platform. The app is published by Asher's Apps. If you have a question about it, write to contact@ashersapps.com.

In short

  • Your budgets are kept on your device.
  • We run no servers, and there is no account with us. We never receive your budgets or anything else about you.
  • The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • If you turn on sync, the app encrypts your budget on your device before it is stored in your own Dropbox account or in a folder you choose.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

What the app keeps on your device

The app stores what you enter: budgets, accounts, transactions, categories, payees and notes. It also stores your name in the app, your time zone and a name for the device (its name or model, used to list your devices when sync is on). All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

Files you import, such as bank statements (QFX or OFX) or a YNAB4 budget, are read on your device. Their contents are added to your budget and are not sent anywhere.

Sync through Dropbox

Sync is optional and off until you turn it on for a budget. It lets you use the same budget on several of your own devices.

  • Signing in. You sign in to Dropbox in your browser. The app never sees your Dropbox password. It receives a sign-in token, which it keeps in your device's secure storage. Signing out in the app cancels the token at Dropbox and removes it from the device.
  • What the app can reach. The app has access only to its own folder in your Dropbox, Apps/Asher's Budget. It cannot see or change any other file in your Dropbox.
  • Your Dropbox name and email address. The app reads them from Dropbox only to show which account you are signed in to. It does not store or send them anywhere.
  • What is stored in your Dropbox. Each shared budget is a folder of files, with a name you choose. The budget's data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is uploaded, using a key made from the budget's passphrase. The passphrase is shown only on your devices and is never uploaded. One small settings file per budget is not encrypted: it holds random ids for the budget and for you, when sharing started, your name in the app, your time zone, and the values a device needs to check the passphrase (they do not contain it).
  • Dropbox's own policy. Files in your Dropbox are kept by Dropbox under its own terms and privacy policy. Asher's Apps is not affiliated with Dropbox.

Sync through a folder on your computer

On a computer you can sync through a folder you choose instead. The app writes the same encrypted files there, and whatever program keeps that folder in step across your computers handles them under its own terms.

Camera

On a phone the app uses the camera only while you scan a budget's pairing code. On a computer you can choose a picture of the code instead. Either image is read on the device and is not saved or sent.

Device backups

On Android, if backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's budgets in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. The Dropbox sign-in token is never included. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Removing your data

  • Uninstalling the app and clearing its data removes your budgets from that device.
  • Deleting a budget's folder in Apps/Asher's Budget in your Dropbox removes the synced copy.
  • You can remove the app's access to your Dropbox at any time in your Dropbox account's settings, under connected apps.

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
